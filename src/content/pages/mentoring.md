## How This Mini-Book Can Help You

Who taught you how to mentor? Probably nobody. One day you're the person asking the questions, and the next you're the person other people ask. It happens slowly and without ceremony, and you don't get a manual. You figure it out by doing it, and by remembering what worked and what didn't when you were on the other side.

This mini-book is my attempt at writing that manual.

It covers mentoring in tech, but most of it applies anywhere. The challenge is the same: you know something, someone else needs to learn it, and the gap between those two is as much trust and timing as knowledge: knowing when to push and when to wait.

If you've ever been responsible for someone else's growth, formally or because they just started asking you things, this is for you.
