## How This Mini-Book Can Help You

Starting on a new team, or just want to sharpen your day-to-day messaging? This mini-book is a shortcut: platforms, phrases, templates, and what to say when production breaks.

The first two chapters cover where most conversations happen: Slack and Microsoft Teams. The rest works on any platform: everyday tech vocabulary, templates you can copy and paste, and how to communicate when production breaks.

Pick the chapter that matches what you need right now, or read them in order.
