## Structure

Making an announcement may not seem so easy when you have to do it out of nowhere. But luckily there is a pattern that you can use to make things better:

- Title: use emojis to catch the attention.
  - 📣 EHQ Announces Internet 2!
  - 📣 Exciting News!
- Identify the Team
- Words of Introduction
- Features/Wins/Gains
- Invitation to do something
- Thanks
- References and Links
- Attached Image/Video:
